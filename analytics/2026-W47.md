## Takeaways
- Audience is still tiny (1–5 accounts reached per post), so differences are directional only.
- Best day: 10 Nov Hong Kong exports (oversized numeral, cobalt) reached 5 accounts on every language; best single post: 11 Nov FA China October trade (15 views, 1 like, 2 shares, the week's only shares).
- Persian is the only language with likes/shares (2 likes, 2 shares); keep FA captions especially strong.
- Posts published around 08:40 Beijing time (9–12 Nov) averaged ~3.3 reach; evening posts (13–15 Nov, ~19:30–20:40) averaged ~1.3. Possible timing effect (older posts also had more days to accumulate); worth testing a morning slot (schedule change is Ali's call).
