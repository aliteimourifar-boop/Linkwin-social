Can you read 7202.41? Every traded product has a code like this.

HS codes, decoded:
• 72 = Chapter: iron and steel
• 7202 = Heading: ferro-alloys
• 7202.41 = Subheading: ferro-chromium, more than 4% carbon

Same heading, different alloys: 7202.11 ferro-manganese (>2% carbon), 7202.21 ferro-silicon (>55% silicon), 7202.30 ferro-silico-manganese.

The Harmonized System has 5,000+ commodity groups, is used by 200+ countries and economies as the basis for their customs tariffs, and covers over 98% of goods in world trade.

Tip: after six digits, codes go national. Confirm the full local code with your customs broker before you ship.
