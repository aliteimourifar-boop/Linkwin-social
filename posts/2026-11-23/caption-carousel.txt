EN | China–Europe freight trains now run full in both directions.
Jan–Oct 2026: 18,661 trains (+14.6% y/y) carried 1.865M TEU (+11.2%). Return trains (9,360) outnumbered outbound (9,301), and the loaded-container ratio has held at 100% for 50 straight months. The trans-Caspian line now runs daily, and Wuhan sent its 500th train of 2026 to Moscow on 20 Nov.
