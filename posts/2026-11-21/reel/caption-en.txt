FOB, CIF or DDP? Who pays for what, and when does the risk become yours?

• FOB (Free On Board): the seller clears the goods for export and loads them on board in China. You pay ocean freight, insurance and import duties. Risk passes once the goods are on board.
• CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight): the seller pays freight and minimum insurance to your port, but the risk still passes to you once the goods are on board in China.
• DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): the seller delivers to your door, cleared for import with duties paid. You unload.

Pro tip: shipping in containers? FOB and CIF are sea rules for goods loaded on board; ICC guidance points to FCA and CIP when containers are handed to the carrier at a terminal.
