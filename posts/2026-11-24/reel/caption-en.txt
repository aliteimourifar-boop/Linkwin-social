Paying a supplier you've never met? A letter of credit lets the banks hold the promise.

Letter of credit (L/C), in 5 steps:
1. You apply: your bank issues an L/C in your seller's favour.
2. Your bank promises: the L/C reaches the seller's bank. Pay if the documents comply.
3. Seller ships, then presents the documents (by default within 21 days of shipment, and before the L/C expires).
4. Banks check: each bank has up to 5 banking days to examine them.
5. The seller is paid; you get the documents to collect the goods.

The twist: banks deal with documents, not goods (UCP 600, Article 5).
Tip: make an inspection certificate one of the required documents, so quality is checked before the bank pays.
