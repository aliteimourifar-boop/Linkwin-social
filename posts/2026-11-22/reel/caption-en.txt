20ft, 40ft or 40ft High Cube? Four numbers decide the box 📦

20′ Standard: 5.90 m inside, 33.2 m³, max payload 28,130 kg
40′ Standard: 12.03 m inside, 67.7 m³, max payload 28,750 kg
40′ High Cube: 2.70 m inside height, 76.3 m³, max payload 28,600 kg

The twist: a 40′ gives you about twice the space of a 20′, but almost the same weight limit.
• Dense cargo such as ferroalloys and metals usually reaches the weight limit first, so a 20′ often does the job.
• Light, bulky goods fill the space first: go 40′ or 40′ HC.
Also check road weight rules at both ends before you load.

Values are typical examples from Hapag-Lloyd container specifications; boxes vary by manufacturer, so confirm with your carrier.
