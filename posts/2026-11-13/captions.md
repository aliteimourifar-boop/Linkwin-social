APEC leaders meet in Shenzhen next week, and the city's roads will run on special rules for five days. If you have cargo moving in or out of Shenzhen between 16 and 20 November, check your plan now.

What the Shenzhen Public Security Bureau has announced (notice of 3 Nov 2026):
• 16–20 Nov, all day: vehicles carrying hazardous goods may not use any road in the city, expressways included.
• 17–18 Nov, 08:00–20:00: large trucks (heavy and medium trucks, tractor units) are kept off Moon Bay Avenue north of the Guangshen Yanjiang Expressway, Beihuan Avenue west of Longzhu interchange and the Tanglangshan Tunnel. Detours: Guangshen Yanjiang Expressway, the Mawan cross-sea passage or Nanping Expressway.
• 18–19 Nov, 06:00–22:00: light and mini trucks may not enter Futian, Luohu or Nanshan, including the expressways inside those districts. Enclosed light and mini vans are exempt.
• Emergency, medical, fresh-food, repair and municipal vehicles can apply for a temporary pass through the "Shenzhen Traffic Police" WeChat account.

Why it matters: the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting takes place in Shenzhen on 18–19 Nov, with the APEC CEO Summit on 17–18 Nov (Xinhua). Delivery windows in Futian, Luohu and Nanshan will be tight for two days, and small delays can ripple into the rest of the week.

Practical tip: where you can, move small-truck pickups and deliveries in those three districts to 16–17 or 20 Nov, ask your forwarder whether any of your shipments travel as dangerous goods, and add a buffer day to anything due in Shenzhen that week. The city's standing truck rules still apply on top of these measures.
