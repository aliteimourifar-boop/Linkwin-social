TRADE RULES
China export controls · Key dates

Three dates every
metals buyer should watch.

China’s one-year pause on its tougher export controls is running out. Here’s the timeline and what it covers.

Nov 2025

One-year pause

After the Busan summit, Beijing paused its October 2025 controls and its US-only export ban for a year.

10 Nov 2026

Pause on the Oct 2025 package ends

Five rare earths (incl. erbium, holmium), rare-earth equipment and tech, lithium-battery and graphite-anode items. No extension notice published as of 5 Nov.

27 Nov 2026

Pause on the US-only ban ends

Gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard materials.

10 Jan 2027

US–China truce deadline

MOFCOM says the Kuala Lumpur trade arrangement now runs to this date.

Never paused: export licences for gallium, germanium, antimony and seven rare earths, for every destination.
Buyer’s tip

Importing critical minerals or magnets from China? Confirm your supplier’s export-licence status before paying a deposit, and allow extra lead time on winter orders.
