Setting up in the Greater Bay Area

Hong Kong or
Guangzhou company?

Many traders need both. Here’s what each one is really for.

Hong Kong

香港
Best for international trade
  • Profits tax 8.25% on the first HK$2M, 16.5% above
  • No VAT or sales tax on goods
  • Free movement of capital in USD, HKD, RMB
  • Common-law contracts trusted by overseas buyers

Guangzhou

广州
Best for selling inside China
  • Issue official fapiao invoices to Chinese clients
  • Standard VAT 13% on most goods
  • Corporate income tax 25%, lower for small firms
  • Hire local staff and sign leases in your own name
PRO TIP

Sell abroad through Hong Kong, buy and sell in China through Guangzhou. Keep the two sets of books separate.
