Sawasdee, Guangzhou! 🙏 Welcome to Thai Narak 蕉媚泰国餐厅 (西门口店).

We cook Thai food by hand (纯手工泰菜) in the heart of Liwan, a short walk from Ximenkou Station on Metro Line 1. This year, Thai Narak was honoured in the 2026 Thai SELECT Promotion Campaign as 2026年度泰精选推广活动 · 优秀泰餐厅 (Outstanding Thai Restaurant). We're grateful to every guest who has sat at our tables, and on Dianping 大众点评 our guests have put us at 荔湾区泰国菜回头客榜 第1名.

Come in for Tom Yum Goong, Green Curry Chicken, Signature Grilled Pork Neck or Coconut Mango Sticky Rice, and stay for the warm light and rattan booths.
